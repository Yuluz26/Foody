Kaunter untuk pelanggan yang datang sendiriA counter for people who walk in

Pelanggan di depan kaunter, tiada telefon di tangan. Staf ketik gambar hidangan, pilih bungkus atau meja yang kosong, terima bayaran tunai atau QR dan lihat baki duit untuk dipulangkan. Pesanan terus masuk ke dapur, stok dan bahan turun macam biasa, dan resit boleh dicetak. Nama dan nombor telefon tak wajib. Berfungsi di tablet, telefon dan komputer.The customer is at the counter with no phone out. Staff tap a dish photo, choose takeaway or a free table, take cash or QR and see the change to give back. The order goes straight to the kitchen, stock and ingredients drop as usual, and the receipt can be printed. Name and phone number are optional. Works on a tablet, a phone and a computer.

Stok makananDish stock

Baki ditolak sendiri bila pesanan masuk dan dipulangkan bila dibatalkan. Tambah stok, catat yang terbuang atau betulkan baki lepas kira rak, semuanya tercatat siapa dan bila. Hidangan hampir habis muncul di Ringkasan. Pelanggan nampak terus bila sesuatu sudah habis. Bila sesuatu habis atau hampir habis, panel beri bunyi dan pop-up, walaupun anda sedang di halaman lain.The count drops by itself when an order comes in and returns when it's cancelled. Add stock, log waste or correct the count after checking the shelf, all recorded with who and when. Dishes running low show up on the summary. Guests see at once when something is gone. When something runs out or runs low, the panel chimes and pops up, even if you are on another page.

Resipi dan kosRecipes and cost

Untuk setiap hidangan, tulis berapa beras, ayam atau santan yang digunakan untuk satu hidangan. Bila hidangan dijual, bahan ditolak sendiri daripada stok bahan. Bila pesanan dibatalkan, bahan dipulangkan. Anda juga nampak kos bahan setiap hidangan dan berapa baki selepas bahan, supaya harga jual tak dianggar-anggar.For each dish, write how much rice, chicken or coconut milk goes into one portion. When the dish sells, the ingredients come off the stock by themselves. If the order is cancelled, they go back. You also see what each dish costs in ingredients and what is left after them, so prices stop being guesswork.

Stok bahanIngredient stock

Senarai bahan di dapur dengan harga seunit, baki dan nilai stok. Staf catat bila beli, guna atau terbuang. Bahan yang ada dalam resipi turun sendiri bila hidangan dijual, dan setiap pergerakan ditunjuk bersama nombor pesanannya. Kalau baki jadi negatif, maksudnya bahan sudah dipakai tetapi pembelian belum dicatat.The kitchen's shelf list with a price per unit, the count and the stock value. Staff log what was bought, used or thrown out. Ingredients in a recipe drop by themselves when a dish sells, and every movement shows its order number. A negative count means the ingredient was used but the purchase was never logged.
